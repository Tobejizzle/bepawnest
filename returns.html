Returns & refunds

Last updated: January 2026

We want you to love what you ordered. If you don't, returning it is simple.

Our 30-day promise

You can return any item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price. Items should be unused, in their original condition, and in the original packaging where possible.

How to start a return

  1. Email support@hearthly.example with your order number and the item you'd like to return.
  2. We'll reply within 24 hours with a return address and simple instructions.
  3. Ship the item back to us. We'll email you as soon as it arrives.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll approve your refund and issue it to your original PayPal or card payment method. Refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Faulty, damaged, or wrong items

If your item arrives faulty, damaged, or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo. We'll arrange a free replacement or a full refund — including any return postage — at no cost to you.

Return shipping costs

For change-of-mind returns, you're responsible for the cost of return shipping. For faulty, damaged, or incorrect items, we cover it.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene reasons, certain personal-care items cannot be returned once opened, unless they are faulty. This will be noted clearly on the product page where it applies.

Cancellations

Need to cancel? Email us as soon as possible. If your order hasn't shipped yet, we'll cancel it and refund you in full. Once an order has shipped, our standard return process applies.

Questions

We're here to help — reach us any time at support@hearthly.example.
