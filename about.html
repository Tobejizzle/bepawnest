About Hearthly

A small team with a simple idea.

Hearthly started with a frustration most people share: shopping online means wading through thousands of near-identical listings, with no idea which ones are actually any good. We wanted the opposite — a short, honest shortlist of products that genuinely make daily life a little better.

So that's what we built. We carry a handful of items at a time. Each one is tested by our team, chosen for everyday usefulness, and backed by a no-nonsense 30-day return policy. If something stops earning its place, we drop it. No clutter, no filler.

What we believe

  • Fewer, better things. Curation beats endless choice.
  • Honest shipping. Tracked delivery is free on every order, and you'll always get a tracking link.
  • Easy to reach. A real person answers our support inbox, usually within 24 hours.
  • Risk-free trial. Try anything for 30 days. Don't love it? Send it back for a refund.

How shipping works

Our products are dispatched from fulfilment partners and shipped directly to you, which lets us keep prices fair and the range tight. Most orders arrive within 7–12 business days. Full details are on our shipping page.

Questions?

We'd love to hear from you. Reach the team any time at support@hearthly.example or through our contact page.
