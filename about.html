About BePawnest

The honest pet shop — and yes, the name is on purpose.

BePawnest started with a simple frustration: shopping for your pet online is a minefield. Endless near-identical listings, fake "80% off" timers, glowing reviews you can't trust, and gadgets that fall apart after a week. Our dogs and cats deserve better than that — and so do the people who love them.

So we built the opposite. The name is a play on be honest, and it's the whole business model: we carry a short, hand-picked range, test everything ourselves, and only stock gear that genuinely solves a problem. Fair prices, no dark-pattern nonsense, and a no-drama 30-day return policy. If something stops earning its place, we drop it.

What we believe

  • Honesty first. Real photos, real descriptions, and prices without the fake-discount theatre.
  • Fewer, better things. A tight, vetted range beats an overwhelming catalogue every time.
  • Free, tracked shipping. On every order, with a tracking link the moment it ships.
  • Easy to reach. A real person answers our inbox, usually within 24 hours.
  • Risk-free. Try anything for 30 days. If your pet isn't into it, send it back for a refund.

How shipping works

Our products are dispatched from vetted fulfilment partners and shipped directly to you, which lets us keep prices fair and the range focused. Most orders arrive within 7–12 business days. Full details are on our shipping page.

Questions?

We'd genuinely love to hear from you — and from your pet. Reach the team any time at support@bepawnest.com or through our contact page.
