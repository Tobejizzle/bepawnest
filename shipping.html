Shipping policy

Last updated: January 2026

We want you to know exactly what to expect after you place an order. Here's how shipping works at BePawnest.

Order processing

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days of being placed. You'll receive an order confirmation by email immediately, and a separate email with tracking once your parcel is dispatched. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next business day.

Delivery estimates

  • United States & Canada: 7–12 business days
  • United Kingdom & Europe: 6–10 business days
  • Australia & New Zealand: 8–14 business days
  • Rest of world: 10–18 business days

These are estimates, not guarantees. Customs processing, weather, and carrier delays can occasionally extend delivery times.

Shipping cost

Standard tracked shipping is free on every order, with no minimum spend. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

Tracking your order

As soon as your order ships, we'll email you a tracking number and link. If you haven't received tracking within 5 business days, please contact us at support@hearthly.example.

A note on how we ship

Some items ship directly from our fulfilment partners. This keeps our range focused and our prices fair, and occasionally means items in the same order arrive separately — at no extra cost to you.

Wrong or undeliverable address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We're not able to reroute a parcel once it has shipped. If a parcel is returned to us as undeliverable, we'll contact you to arrange reshipment or a refund.

Questions

Email us any time at support@hearthly.example and we'll help.
