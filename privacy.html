Privacy policy

Last updated: January 2026

Hearthly ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Order information: your name, shipping address, email, and order details when you make a purchase.
  • Payment information: payments are processed securely by PayPal. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Communications: messages you send us through our contact form or by email.
  • Usage data: basic analytics such as pages visited and device type, collected through cookies to help us improve the site and measure our advertising.

How we use your information

  • To process, fulfil, and deliver your orders.
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and respond to support requests.
  • To improve our website, products, and customer experience.
  • To measure and improve our advertising, including through Google Ads and Google Analytics.
  • To send marketing emails, only where you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing your information

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run the store — for example payment processors (PayPal), shipping and fulfilment partners, email providers, and analytics/advertising providers (such as Google). We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & consent

We use cookies to keep your shopping cart working, remember preferences, understand how the site is used, and measure advertising performance. When required, we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies, and you can decline. You can also disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

Data retention

We keep order records for as long as necessary to fulfil orders, comply with legal and tax obligations, and resolve disputes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@hearthly.example.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted checkout via PayPal. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

Children

Our website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email support@hearthly.example.
